## 1. Identity

**Object Name:** Charge

**Namespace:** Billing

**Parent Object:** Billing: Journal

**ID Prefix:** CHG

**Description:**
A Charge is a single billing entry within a Billing: [[Journal]] — one line of a Vendor's normalized billing data for a subscription-based [[Product]]. Each entry records what is being billed (a usage period, quantity, and purchase price supplied by the Vendor) and, once the platform processes the parent Journal, the matched Marketplace identities and derived commercial values: the [[Seller]], [[Authorization]], [[Buyer]], client and vendor [[Account]]s, [[Item]], the [[Subscription]], [[Agreement]] or [[Asset]] the entry resolves to, the [[Licensee]], and the confidential sell-side pricing. Charges are generated by the platform from the Journal's uploaded data — they are never created, edited, or deleted directly through the API — and they are the atomic unit that rating turns into Ledger charges and, ultimately, the Statement lines that bill a Client. A charge whose matched Subscription or Agreement has active split billing (via [[Subscription Split Billing]] or [[Agreement Split Billing]]) is automatically divided into child charges, one per allocation.

**Also Known As:**
Charge; billing charge; journal entry; journal line. A Charge that carries an allocation of a split parent is informally a "split child" charge.

## 2. Ownership & Visibility

| Actor      | Can Create | Can Read | Can Update | Can Delete | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vendor     | No | Yes (restricted fields) | No | No | Reads charges on its own Journals. Confidential sell-side pricing and the Operations processing status are suppressed — see Section 5 and BR-011. |
| Operations | No | Yes (all fields) | Yes (reconciliation only) | No | The only Actor permitted the `match` / `ignore` / `reset` reconciliation actions (BR-002); these edit a limited set of fields on a matched charge. |
| Client     | No | No | No | No | Journal-scoped charges are not readable by the Client Actor (BR-012). Clients see billing charges only through Statements. |

## 3. State Machine

A Charge carries a status drawn from a single set of values, surfaced through two per-Actor views: an `upload` status (Vendor-visible) reflecting the upload and validation outcome, and a `processing` status (Operations-visible) reflecting the reconciliation resolution (see BR-013). The state machine below is expressed over that status. Charges have no direct creation, update, or deletion endpoints; they are generated, recomputed, and removed as side effects of processing the parent Journal, plus three Operations reconciliation actions.

### 3.1 States

| State | Description | Initial State? | Terminal State? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ready | Validated and resolved to a billable identity; eligible for rating and billing. | Yes | No |
| Error | The entry failed validation or its Subscription/Agreement/Asset/Item could not be resolved; excluded from billing until corrected. | No | No |
| Split | A parent charge that has been divided into child charges (one per split-billing allocation); carries no billable amount of its own. | No | No |
| Skipped | Intentionally excluded from automated billing because a manual billing override applies for the charge's vendor/client. | No | No |
| Ignored | Manually excluded from billing by an Operations `ignore` action; its Ledger and Statement links are cleared. | No | No |
| Reconciling | An Operations reconciliation (`match` or `reset`) has been applied and is pending the next Journal recalculation, which recomputes the status. | No | No |

### 3.2 Transitions

| ID | From State | To State | Action | Endpoint / Verb | Actor | Precondition | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T1 | — | Ready | Create | system (automatic) | System (Journal submitter's context) | Parent Journal submitted and its uploaded data processed | Generated from the Journal's data; there is no dedicated charge creation endpoint. |
| T2 | Ready | Error | Flag validation/identity error | system (automatic) | System | Enrichment cannot validate the entry or resolve its matched object | Error detail carried in the `upload`/`processing` error lists. |
| T3 | Ready | Split | Split into child charges | system (automatic) | System | Matched Subscription or Agreement has active split billing with allocations | One child charge is created per allocation; the parent moves to Split (BR-014). |
| T4 | Ready | Skipped | Skip via manual override | system (automatic) | System | A manual billing override applies for the charge's vendor/client | Excluded from automated billing. |
| T5 | Ready | Reconciling | Match | `match` | Operations | Charge not in Split; parent Journal in Review or Reconciling | Re-points the charge to an Operations-chosen Subscription, Agreement, or Asset (BR-006/BR-007). |
| T6 | Error | Reconciling | Match | `match` | Operations | Charge not in Split; parent Journal in Review or Reconciling | Resolves an errored charge by matching it to the correct object. |
| T7 | Ready | Ignored | Ignore | `ignore` | Operations | Charge not in Split or already Ignored; parent Journal in Review or Reconciling | Clears the charge's Ledger and Statement links (BR-016). |
| T8 | Error | Ignored | Ignore | `ignore` | Operations | Charge not in Split or already Ignored; parent Journal in Review or Reconciling | — |
| T9 | Reconciling | Ignored | Ignore | `ignore` | Operations | Charge not in Split or already Ignored; parent Journal in Review or Reconciling | — |
| T10 | Ignored | Reconciling | Reset | `reset` | Operations | Charge previously reconciled and not already reset; parent Journal in Review or Reconciling | Restores the charge's originally computed values. Reset also reverts a prior Match (the charge stays Reconciling, pending recalculation). |
| T11 | Reconciling | Ready | Resolve on recalculation | system (automatic) | System | Parent Journal recalculated or regenerated and the charge re-enriched successfully | The same recompute may instead resolve the charge to Error, Split, or Skipped. |

### 3.3 State Diagram

## 4. Business Rules

| Rule ID | Rule Statement | Applies In State(s) | Actor Scope | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BR-001 | A Charge belongs to exactly one Billing: [[Journal]] and is generated from that Journal's uploaded data; it cannot be created, updated, or deleted directly through the API. | All | All | Read-only endpoints (list and by-id) plus the three reconciliation actions are the only API surface. |
| BR-002 | The `match`, `ignore`, and `reset` reconciliation actions are permitted only to the Operations Actor. | Ready, Error, Reconciling, Ignored | Operations | — |
| BR-003 | A reconciliation action is permitted only while the parent Journal is in its Review or Reconciling state; performing any of them moves that Journal to Reconciling. | All | Operations | See Section 7.2. Journal states are not yet canonised. |
| BR-004 | A charge in Split status cannot be matched, ignored, or reset. | Split | Operations | A Split charge is a parent divided into child charges; reconcile the children instead. |
| BR-005 | `ignore` requires the charge is not already Ignored; `reset` requires the charge was previously reconciled and has not already been reset. | Ready, Error, Reconciling, Ignored | Operations | — |
| BR-006 | A `match` must specify exactly one target — a Subscription, an Agreement, or an Asset. | Ready, Error, Reconciling | Operations | Supplying zero or more than one of the three is rejected. |
| BR-007 | A `match` target's Authorization must be the same as the parent Journal's Authorization. | Ready, Error, Reconciling | Operations | Matched [[Subscription]], [[Agreement]], or [[Asset]] must share the Journal's [[Authorization]]. |
| BR-008 | A Subscription with active split billing cannot be used as a `match` target. | Ready, Error, Reconciling | Operations | Split billing is applied automatically during enrichment (BR-014), not via manual match. |
| BR-009 | Matching to an Agreement requires that Agreement to have a default markup. | Ready, Error, Reconciling | Operations | Without a default markup the sell-side price cannot be derived. |
| BR-010 | A Line supplied with a `match` must belong to the matched Subscription or Asset. | Ready, Error, Reconciling | Operations | The Line identifies the specific [[Order]] line within the matched object. |
| BR-011 | The confidential sell-side pricing fields and the Operations processing status are not visible to the Vendor Actor. | All | Vendor | Reference preamble §6.3. Covers unit and total sale price, buyer-currency sale price, markup, markup source, margin, sale currency, and the exchange rate. |
| BR-012 | The Client Actor cannot read a Journal-scoped charge; charges become Client-visible only through Statements. | All | Client | A direct read returns Forbidden. Statement is not yet canonised. |
| BR-013 | A charge carries two per-Actor status views: `upload` (Vendor-visible), reflecting the upload and validation outcome, and `processing` (Operations-visible), reflecting the reconciliation resolution. | All | All | Both draw from the same value set: Ready, Error, Split, Skipped, Ignored, Reconciling. Each view also carries its own error list. |
| BR-014 | When a charge's matched Subscription or Agreement has active split billing, the platform automatically divides it into one child charge per allocation, and the parent moves to Split. | Ready | System | Each child carries its allocation percentage, its split-billing [[Buyer]], and pricing scaled by that percentage; the parent retains no billable amount. See [[Subscription Split Billing]] and [[Agreement Split Billing]]. |
| BR-015 | A Charge is always of Automated billing type. | All | All | The billing type value set also includes Manual (originating from a Manual Upload, not yet canonised) and Consolidated (origin unconfirmed — see CHG-001); neither arises for a Charge. |
| BR-016 | Ignoring a charge removes it from billing and clears its Ledger and Statement links; `reset` restores the charge's originally computed matching. | Ready, Error, Reconciling, Ignored | Operations | Reset draws on the values captured when the charge was first calculated. |
| BR-017 | On Journal recalculation the processing status of every charge is recomputed; on Journal regeneration the entire charge set is discarded and rebuilt from the Journal's uploaded data. | All | System | Regeneration does not preserve prior reconciliation; charges are re-derived from source. |

## 7. Lifecycle Events & Side Effects

### 7.1 Internal Events

| Event | Trigger | Permitted Actor(s) | Side Effect / Downstream Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Charge generated | Parent Journal submitted and its data processed | System (Journal submitter's context) | Charge created and enriched; status resolved to Ready, Error, Split, or Skipped. |
| Charge matched | `match` action | Operations | Re-points the charge to a chosen [[Subscription]], [[Agreement]], or [[Asset]]; records a reconciliation stamp; sets processing status to Reconciling. |
| Charge ignored | `ignore` action | Operations | Sets processing status to Ignored; clears the charge's Ledger and Statement links; records a reconciliation stamp. |
| Charge reset | `reset` action | Operations | Restores originally computed values; sets processing status to Reconciling; records a reset stamp. |

### 7.2 Cross-Object State Effects

| Triggering Event | Affected Object | Effect on Affected Object | Automated? | Condition | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Charge matched / ignored / reset | Journal | Journal status set to Reconciling | Yes (Operations token) | Journal was in Review or Reconciling | The reconciliation action drives the parent Journal into its Reconciling state. |
| Charge split | Charge | Child charges created, one per allocation | Yes (Journal submitter's context) | Matched Subscription/Agreement has active split billing | Parent moves to Split and retains no billable amount. |
| Charge rated | Ledger | Charge referenced into a Ledger charge for its Seller | Yes | Parent Journal accepted and rated | Ledger not yet canonised. |
| Charge billed | Statement | Charge referenced into the Statement for its Journal, Agreement, and Buyer | Yes | Charge is billable (not Ignored/Skipped/Error) | Statement not yet canonised. |

## 8. Reversibility & Data Retention

**Reversible transitions:**
An [[Agreement]], [[Subscription]], or [[Asset]] match and an `ignore` are both reversible by an Operations `reset`, which restores the charge's originally computed values (the charge then returns to Reconciling, pending recalculation). The Error and Ready states move between each other freely as the source data is corrected and the Journal is recalculated. The Split state of a parent charge is not reversed by any action — it is undone only when the parent Journal is regenerated, which rebuilds the whole charge set. Reconciling is a transient, pending state resolved by the next recalculation.

**Deletion:**
A Charge cannot be deleted directly in any state — there is no deletion endpoint. Charges are permanently removed — no longer retrievable via the API — when the parent Journal is regenerated (the entire charge set is discarded and rebuilt) or when the parent Journal is itself removed. Consistent with Platform Invariant 6, a charge is never removed as a side effect of deleting a matched [[Subscription]], [[Agreement]], [[Asset]], or any other referenced object; those references become stale snapshots rather than triggering removal.

**Audit & history requirements:**
A charge carries in-object audit stamps recording the Actor and timestamp of its most recent reconcile and reset. The platform does not emit separate Audit Records (Audit namespace) for charge events — charge auditing is limited to these in-object stamps, and the automatic split writes no stamp at all. Prior reconciliation state is not retained across a Journal regeneration — the charge set is re-derived from source data.

## 9. Failure Modes & Edge Cases

| Scenario | Expected System Behavior | Actor Impacted | Risk Level | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| An Error charge is left unresolved when the Journal is accepted | The entry is excluded from billing; the corresponding usage is not billed to the Client | Operations | Medium | Under-billing until a corrected Journal is regenerated. |
| Operations matches a charge to the wrong [[Subscription]] or [[Agreement]] | The platform bills the matched object; the charge resolves to Ready against an incorrect target | Client | High | The Authorization guard (BR-007) narrows but does not eliminate mis-matching within an Authorization. |
| A matched Subscription has active split billing but no valid allocations | The charge is flagged with a processing error ("no split allocations found") and excluded from billing | Operations | Medium | Requires the [[Subscription Split Billing]] configuration to be corrected before recalculation. |
| A referenced object (e.g. matched [[Subscription]]) changes after the charge is calculated | The charge holds a snapshot; the change is reflected only on the next Journal recalculation | Operations | Medium | No automatic propagation between recalculations. |
| Vendor cannot see the processing status or sell-side pricing | The Vendor sees only the `upload` status; Operations-side resolution and margins are invisible to it | Vendor | Low | By design — reference preamble §6.3 and BR-011. |
